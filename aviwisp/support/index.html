Mac app support

Help for your next desktop flight.

Aviwisp turns your Mac desktop into a journey over satellite imagery, following historical flights. Here is how to get started and get help.

macOS 26.4+No account requiredHistorical flight visualization

Getting started

Open Aviwisp and wait for the historical archive and satellite map to load. No account is required. Use Random Flight to choose another flight, or More > Choose a Flight… to select an available date, flight and UTC time.

Desktop wallpaper

Use Set as Wallpaper or the menu-bar controls to display the scene on connected screens. Click the Dock icon or Open Aviwisp in the menu-bar menu to reopen the main window. Turn wallpaper mode off to restore your desktop.

Camera and appearance

Open More > Flight Details & Appearance to choose Close-up, Chase, Scenic or Automatic camera views. You can adjust aircraft appearance and turn nearby landmark labels on or off.

Historical visualization

The app uses archived departure and arrival records to simulate paths and altitude. It does not show live aircraft positions and must not be used for navigation or operational flight decisions.

Map loading

Use the toolbar to choose Satellite or Standard. Satellite imagery and place names depend on the network, service coverage and system region. If satellite imagery stays blank or blurry, try Standard; your choice is saved and applies to the preview and desktop. A missing map does not mean a flight is unavailable.

Sending feedback

Use Settings > Support > Email Support, or contact ws.feedback@outlook.com. Recent diagnostic logs are included in the prepared email draft by default; you can turn that option off, review or remove the attachment, and decide whether to send the message. The app does not send it automatically.

Sharing diagnostic logs

Settings > Support > Export Diagnostic Logs… saves a local file you can share manually. Logs can include system uptime, system and display details, app settings, historical flight selection, server names and performance information. Review before sharing. Local logs are retained for up to 14 days and 40 MiB total; older records may be removed sooner.

Requirements

macOS 26.4 or later. The app interface is currently English. Internet access is required for historical archive downloads, satellite imagery and online place-name lookups.

Flight data and licensing

Historical records are adapted from MrAirspace / adsb.lol under ODbL 1.0. See the flight-data license and free complete database downloads for credits, transformation notes and coverage limits. Aircraft use illustrative family models and original fictional paint.
