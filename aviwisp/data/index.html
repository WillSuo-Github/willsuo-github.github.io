Get the complete adapted database

All published dates and every complete daily document are offered free of charge, without an account, authentication or a purchase of Aviwisp. Download every date from the machine-readable date catalog using https://aviwisp-history.open-live-catalog.workers.dev/v1/history/days/YYYY-MM-DD. Documents are gzip-encoded JSON; web clients normally decompress them automatically. Flights crossing midnight can occur in both dates, with stable IDs.

Changes made for Aviwisp

We retain valid ground-to-ground records with one airport at each end; normalize basic aircraft identity; remove duplicate records; assign stable IDs and representative airport coordinates; partition records into UTC-day documents; and compress the result. We do not copy source photographs, source README images/prose, validation-route fields or detailed aircraft descriptions into this database. Paths and altitudes displayed in the app are simulations calculated from historical endpoints, not recorded flight tracks.
