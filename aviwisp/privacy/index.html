Support

Aviwisp for Mac

Privacy for your desktop journeys.

Local preferences and diagnostics, online maps and historical archives, and support only when you choose to contact us.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes Aviwisp for macOS and its public historical flight archive service, published by WillSuo. Privacy contact: ws.feedback@outlook.com.

No account, advertising or tracking

Aviwisp does not require an account, show advertising, or use cross-app tracking, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not request your device’s location permission to replay historical flights. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Information on your Mac

Preferences such as appearance, map style and flight filters are saved in the app’s local container. Downloaded archive files may be cached by macOS networking. Temporary place-name caches are held locally in memory. Local diagnostic logs can include app/build and macOS versions, locale/time zone, display configuration, settings, app-launch events, flight selections, setting changes, server names, request outcomes, system uptime and performance observations. A random diagnostic-session identifier groups local log events; it is not used for advertising or a cross-app profile.

Local logs and optional support

Local logs are retained for up to 14 days, with a 5 MiB limit per file and a 40 MiB total limit; older records can be removed sooner. Logs are not uploaded automatically. Email Support opens an email draft. Its optional recent-log attachment can be reviewed or removed before you choose to send the message. Export Diagnostic Logs saves a file you may share manually. Credential/path redaction is best effort; review reports before sharing.

If you contact support, we receive the sender name/address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These may include diagnostic and performance information. We use them to respond, investigate problems and maintain the service. We retain support correspondence and attachments for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your request, investigate and resolve the issue, and meet applicable legal obligations. We delete or anonymize material when it is no longer needed. Access is limited to handling support and operating the service. You can request deletion by contacting the support address. Copies retained independently by your email provider, Discord, or backup services follow their policies.

Historical archive downloads and Cloudflare

The app downloads a date catalog and daily historical flight files from our service hosted by Cloudflare. Requests identify the requested archive date and carry ordinary connection information such as IP address, user agent and request timing. The app does not add an account ID, advertising ID, persistent installation ID or your device’s GPS coordinates. Our Worker does not persist visitor IP addresses, request headers or a browsing history, and its persistent invocation logs, Logpush and tail receivers are disabled. Cloudflare may process and retain network/security information under its own privacy policy to deliver, secure and operate its services. Disabling our application logs does not mean Cloudflare performs no data processing or that we control every internal retention period.

Apple map services

MapKit supplies map imagery and reverse-geocoded place names. Requests concern the displayed map area and the simulated flight’s coordinates, not a GPS location requested by Aviwisp. Apple and applicable regional map providers process the technical and map-request information needed for these services under Apple’s Maps privacy information. Map availability and detail can vary by region. We do not receive Apple’s individual map-service logs or build a personal map-use history.

Email, community and this website

Support email is handled through the email services used by you and the support recipient. Discord is an optional external community; your posts, profile and attachments are handled by Discord and can be visible to other community members according to where you post. These website pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes connection information under the GitHub privacy statement. The Aviwisp pages do not add advertising or analytics scripts. Each provider’s own security, backup, legal and retention practices may apply.

Your choices and requests

You can choose which flights to replay, change appearance and map style, stop wallpaper playback, remove email attachments, and decide whether to share a support report. Removing the app’s local container removes its local preferences and files; exported files, email drafts, backups and copies you separately share may remain. Contact ws.feedback@outlook.com to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, or ask about applicable privacy rights. We may need information sufficient to verify the request and may retain material required by law. You can also contact the relevant provider about information it independently handles.

International processing, children and updates

Network, hosting, email and community services may process information in countries different from yours under their applicable terms and safeguards. Aviwisp does not intentionally solicit personal information from children; contact us if a child has sent support information that should be deleted. This policy will be updated when the app’s data handling changes. The date at the bottom identifies the current version.
